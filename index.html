Custom Woodworking & Processing

Milling & Processing Services

We process raw logs directly into ready-to-use construction and woodworking material.

Custom Dimensional Sawing

Precision bandsaw cutting to non-standard or large timber specifications. Post sizes up to 12" x 12" and lengths up to 24 feet.

Surfacing & S4S Planing

Smooth Surfaced 4 Sides (S4S) or S2S planing to ensure exact uniform thickness and width for joinery and cabinet making.

Kiln Drying (KD)

Controlled chamber moisture reduction down to 8% – 12% moisture content to prevent splitting, checking, or warping during export.

Live Edge Slabs

Book-matched live edge timber slabs cut from Mahogany, Santa Maria, and Guanacaste logs for custom dining tables and island tops.

Export Stacking & Bundling

ISPM 15 heat treatment compliance, protective wrapping, and container strapping optimized for dispatch through Big Creek Port or Bomba Dock.

Logistics & Direct Delivery

Flatbed truck delivery service across all Belize districts including Cayo, Stann Creek, Corozal, and Belize City.
